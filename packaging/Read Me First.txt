SUPER GEMINI LIBRARIAN
A preset librarian for the UDO Super Gemini and Super 8.
(An independent tool, not made by UDO Audio.)

INSTALL
1. Drag "Super Gemini Librarian" onto the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. Open it from Applications or Launchpad.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
This app is not registered with Apple, so macOS will say it
"could not verify" it, or that it is from an unidentified developer.
To open it anyway:
  1. Click Done (or Cancel) on that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down. Next to the message about "Super Gemini Librarian",
     click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
On older macOS versions you can instead right-click (Control-click) the
app, choose Open, then click Open.

If macOS asks whether the app may access files on a removable volume,
click Allow - that is how it reaches the synth.

CONNECTING THE SYNTH
1. Switch the Super Gemini off.
2. Hold PATCH and switch it on. Release once the LEDs settle.
3. Connect it to the Mac by USB. A disk named GEMINI appears, and the
   librarian finds it within a few seconds.
4. When finished, click Eject in the librarian, then restart the synth.

Your library is kept in Music > Super Gemini Librarian.
If something goes wrong, the log is in
~/Library/Logs/Super Gemini Librarian.log
