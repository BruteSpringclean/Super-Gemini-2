Library

Drag patches (.usg) or performances (.xsg) here from Finder, or drag a slot from the synth to save it.

    Synth

    Connect your Super Gemini

    1. Switch the Super Gemini off.
    2. Hold the PATCH button and switch it on. Release once the LEDs settle.
    3. Connect USB to your Mac. A disk called GEMINI (or SUPER8) appears.
    4. This page finds it automatically.

    Settings

    Leave empty to look for /Volumes/GEMINI and /Volumes/SUPER8.

    Library folder: Show in Finder

    Automatic backup before sending

    Copies only the banks you are changing to your Mac first. Turn it off to save time if you keep your own copies.

    Synth backups

    Restoring a backup also backs up the current state of those banks first.
